Context

The question and the method

  • Question: After a simulated human passes a recorder, do animals change their calls? Or does the detector only hear the playback?
  • Data: 6 AudioMoth recorders, 5 days, 29 simulated human-presence events (11 isolated, 11 overlapping, 7 incomplete).
  • Measure: Call rate after each event, minus a baseline for the same recorder and time of day. Bins are 15 minutes.
  • Test: Random-intercept model by recorder. The gate is two-sided p below 0.05 for the original 0-60 minute analysis.
  • Controls: A placebo-in-time test (1,000 draws) and variants that remove clips that YAMNet flagged as human sound.
  • Rules fixed first: PREREGISTRATION.md was committed before any variant ran. Every later change is in DEVIATIONS.md.

Results

Result 1: the pre-registered test is inconclusive

Gate fails: p = 0.64. It needs p < 0.05.
  • All six variants give a small drop of 0.34 to 0.58 calls per minute.
  • Every interval includes zero.
  • With 10 events, the test cannot detect changes smaller than 1.6 to 2.6 calls per minute.
  • This is not evidence of no effect.

The sign depends on the baseline exploratory

  • Without baseline adjustment the estimate is +0.68 (p = 0.28, 11 events). With complete windows only it is +0.53 (5 events).
  • Audio_Moth_1 to 4 average +0.66. Audio_Moth_5 and 6 average -1.91. Each group has 5 events.
  • Audio_Moth_5 and 6 have no presence-free days, so their baselines are thin.
3

The placebo test shows no drop exploratory

  • 5 events on 4 recorders. Observed +0.47. The placebo 95% range is -1.36 to +0.47. Two-sided p = 0.054.
  • The one-sided test for a drop gives p = 0.97. The six variants share the same 5 events.
  • YAMNet flagged only 1.6% of simulated clips (168 of 10,636). Mean contrast: -0.36 with a detection, -0.32 without. The split is confounded with recorder.

Review and approval

Three decisions I would like you to review

1

The count model and two extra checks

  • Count model: negative binomial, recorded-minutes offset, recorder fixed effects, standard errors clustered by recorder and date. Six recorders are too few for a random effect.
  • Leave-own-day-out baseline, and a leave-one-recorder-out refit. All three are exploratory and cannot change the verdict.
  • The specifications were committed before any run.
2

The design of the listening review

  • I review 200 clips (100 YAMNet-positive, 100 negative), blind to the label and the position. Order is random.
  • Clips that overlap another simulated sound are excluded (2,400 clips). Only 104 positive clips remain, so the positive sample is almost all of them.
3

  • Permission to keep event_study.py and placebo_hypothesis_analysis.py in the repository.
